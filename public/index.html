Shipping

Orders are prepared once payment is confirmed on Stripe. Items ship from our supplier: typically 5–12 days with expedited shipping or 15–30 days standard, to US addresses.

Import duty may apply on low-value orders, since the US de minimis exemption ended in August 2025.

If an item hasn't arrived within 35 days of payment, email flyaeriq@gmail.com and we'll track it down or make it right.

Returns & refunds

Changed your mind? Email flyaeriq@gmail.com within 30 days of delivery and we'll arrange a return for a refund to your original payment method — items must be unused and in their original packaging, and return shipping is on us if the item arrived damaged or wrong.

Two exceptions: printed books and manuals must be unmarked, and custom items (the printed aviator T-shirt, and CFI endorsement sheets printed with your details) can only be returned if they arrive damaged or with an error. Refunds are issued through Stripe once the return is on its way back.

About AERIQ

AERIQ is run by a working FAA Gold Seal flight instructor in Sacramento, CA. Everything in the shop is gear a CFI actually reaches for: the kneeboard and E6B students train with, the handbooks and ACS booklets checkrides are built on, the uniform pieces instructors wear, and the endorsement labels that save handwriting a paragraph into a cramped logbook margin.
